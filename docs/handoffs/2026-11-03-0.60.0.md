## Decisions

- The move back is found by `com.claim`, not `com.enroll` or `com.responder_replace`: the contract names the claim
  (project-comms.md, PR-2, "Learning the slot is its own"), and an enroll or replace would take a slot another session
  holds, which FAA-155 forbids.
- The probe compares with the checkpoint's recorded `moved` generation, not the in-memory one, and `fenced` or
  another refusal of the claim (not `held`, `lease_expired`, the epoch's two codes or the board session's, `not_authorized` counted as another session's) rewrites
  `moved`: each later generation is asked about once, across restarts too. Each new generation's probe uses a fresh
  claim key; a probe whose answer was lost asks again under its key (seam reading 2), and a recorded move drops the old
  one (seam reading 1).
- No new event for a move back: the operator made it; a later move is said again.
- FAA-172: the requester sees no attempt, so the guidance is to ask on the thread; whether Fabric's thread view should
  mark the case is asked, not assumed.
