## Constraint

The conditional exit contract reaches a Hermes session as one system prompt section, and Hermes caps a plugin's
section at 4000 characters itself (`hermes_cli/plugins_dispatch.py:72`, `MAX_SYSTEM_PROMPT_SECTION_CHARS = 4_000`,
Hermes v0.22.2), so raising `MAX_CONTEXT_CHARS` in the adapter's Hermes plugin buys nothing. 0.67.0 left 2 characters
(3998 at its longest). Every addition below takes its characters back from the contract, moving explanation to
`references/on-demand-session.md`, which the contract names.
