# 0.71.0 brief: the contract pin moves to `v0.1.0+2026-11-06.1`

Pin `eac1a594aa131851f079da8bb7b2149e1951f35f` (`v0.1.0+2026-11-06.1`) from `3830a33` (`v0.1.0+2026-10-27.6`).
Between them: DEC-0140, DEC-0146, DEC-0147, DEC-0148, DEC-0150, DEC-0153. The contract's `package.json` and
`pnpm-lock.yaml` did not change.

## Decisions

- D1. A re-pin only. The channel agent's check (FAA-197) and the operations digest module (FAA-206 part 2) are their
  own releases: both are new code of a size (a schema subset with `oneOf`, `maximum`, `minProperties`,
  `maxProperties`; FAC-SEM-162 to 168) that a re-pin should not carry.
- D2. The vendored vectors are regenerated from a `git archive` of the tag with the contract's own tools: at the old
  pin they come out byte-identical to the committed files, at the new pin only `feed-protocols-vectors.json`
  (`channel-agent-standard` `['1']`, `fabric-ops-digest` `['0.1']`) and `descriptor-fixtures.json`
  (`service-descriptor-ops-digest`) change. Both register twins follow; the descriptor count is 12.
- D3. `3830a33` joins `SUPPORTED_CONTRACT_COMMITS` and the validator's previous pins: bundles issued under it stay
  valid.
- D4. DEC-0147's wording: a human gate is "a secret typed in or handed out" in working-with-fabric (SKILL.md, the exit
  contract, on-demand-session.md, the evals). SKILL.md stays under 4750 words (4749) by dropping articles in the same
  line; the exit contract stays under Hermes' 4000 characters (3999 at its longest) the same way.
- D5. DEC-0150 is named as released (`v0.1.0+2026-11-05.2`) in practices.md and the support-agent recipe. Re-quoting
  CA-8 verbatim is part of FAA-197.
- D6. The register's gain narrows the kits: another revision of either new protocol (`channel-agent-standard/2`) is now
  FAC-SEM-099, where an unknown name passed before. The CHANGELOG says so.
- D7. DEC-0140, DEC-0146, DEC-0148 and DEC-0147's peer-share report reach no kit code; the adapter declares no
  `project.work` (`git grep -nF project.work -- plugins bin test` prints nothing). DEC-0140 PR-2 (the operator's move of a slot) the board kit has
  followed since FAA-155; `references/board.md` now says it is inside the pin.
- D8 (readings round 1). The ops-digest key belongs to a local descriptor, never a well-known answer (service.md at the
  pin): `references/protocol.md` names it in its Descriptor section, and the channel-agent key in the well-known
  section only for a service with no manifest. practices.md and the recipe say `check_service.py` does not read the
  channel-agent declaration yet. SKILL.md's spend gate keeps its meaning ("link and amount handed over, never a chat
  question"). `references/on-demand-session.md` no longer says an `about` settles later questions (Fabric thread
  `1add3c03`, `5b216035`); FAA-241 records the always-ask change Fabric and Vesta take after CO-506.
