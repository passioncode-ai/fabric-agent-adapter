# 0.73.0 brief: the channel agent's declaration in the probe (FAA-197)

Contract pin `eac1a594` (`v0.1.0+2026-11-06.1`, since 0.71.0) carries DEC-0150: the extension key
`https://fabric.passioncode.ai/agent-contract/extensions/channel-agent/0.1`, the schema
`schemas/service-channel-agent-extension.schema.json`, FAC-SEM-162 to 165 (`src/channel-agent.ts`), the vectors
`fixtures/vectors/channel-agent.json` (148 declarations) and `docs/specification/channel-agent.md`. The contract's
handoff `docs/handoffs/2026-11-04-channel-agent.md` (Open, and the posts for FAA-197) names what the adapter owes.

## Decisions

- D1. `check_service.py` reads a channel agent's declaration in both places CA-1 names: `provider.extensions` of the
  manifest the descriptor names (`fabricManifest`), and, for a service with no manifest, `extensions` of its
  well-known answer. A service with a manifest is read from the manifest only; a copy in its well-known answer is
  reported as ignored, never as the declaration.
- D2. The rules are the contract's FAC-SEM-162 to 165, ported to the standard library in a module of the probe
  (`check_service_channel_agent.py`, not a kit module: the probe has no Node twin and `kit.json` does not list it),
  with the schema vendored at the pin and the vectors vendored and run in a test that compares each declaration's
  findings `{code, instancePath}` as a set, as the vectors' `about` allows.
- D3. A declaration the probe reads as not declared (an older form, a key elsewhere) is reported, never a failure,
  while live producers still publish declarations written before this revision; findings on a declared one fail the
  rule.
  Amended 2026-11-07 after the unit reading: «read as not declared» is the contract's reader (CA-2): a declaration
  under the key that the schema or FAC-SEM-163 refuses is `NOT_RUN` with its findings; the rule fails on FAC-SEM-164's
  obligations of a declaration still read, and always on a string shaped like a credential (FAC-SEM-165), in the read
  declaration or in any copy beside it (a copy no reader reads still sits in a published document), which is never a
  compatibility matter.
  Amended again 2026-11-07: this is the contract's own norm, not a choice of the kit. FAC-SEM-165 at the pin reads a
  declaration "a misplaced one included" (DEC-0150 CA-5), and the contract's owner confirmed it on board thread
  `d165f328`.
- D4. A well-known answer shares the 2 MiB cap of Fabric Dashboards' probe (until the contract's CT-223): the rule
  names it when the answer is near or over it.
- D5. `creating-fabric-agents/references/practices.md` quotes CA-8 verbatim from the pinned
  `docs/specification/channel-agent.md`, with its identification bound, "acts only inside that chat" and "no AP-3 or
  OA-2 source", in place of 0.71.0's paraphrase.
