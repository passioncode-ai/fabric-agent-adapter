## Decisions

1. The verifiers follow DEC-0045 exactly, re-proved by regenerating the shared vectors with the contract's verifier at
   the new pin (on Node 22 and 26) and adding cases for every new type rule.
2. The gate takes the caller's tier: `gate(tool, headers, tier="T2")`. With `T0` or `T1` and no token it passes
   (no delegation); a present token is verified at every tier. The service decides the tier (T0: its local floor
   authenticated a loopback caller); a relayed call is T2. `McpToolServer.handle(..., tier=)` passes it through.
3. `issuer` is required in the gate and the verifiers, as in the contract.
4. `refusal_from_denial` / `refusalFromDenial` is ported for a callee that checks grants with the meter.
5. `fabric_provider.py` accepts `run.mcp.stdio.scope` (`project` or `on-demand`; `session` is refused, FAC-SEM-056).
6. Version: next minor (0.12.0).
